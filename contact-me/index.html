About / Contact Me

Hello and thanks for visiting my site! I'm an independent software developer specialising in sports games. My most notable work is Cricket Coach, which had its first release way back in September 2001. Back then the game was published and appeared in a box in shops, these days it's released digitally. Recently the game was re-released and now a single purchase gets you the game on Windows and MacOS.

I also run a software company that I started with my brother in 2010, that has since grown to become significantly bigger than the games company.

I can be contacted via X. I'm interested in meeting people who do similar things.

I'm English but live in the south of Spain with my wife and young son.

The world of technology is changing at a rapid rate, what I do on a day to day basis has changed dramatically from 5 years ago. This blog is my outlet for my thoughts on that change and what the future holds. I'm very optimistic about the future that we're rapidly moving towards, I'm hopeful that my blog articles help people in some way, or at least provide entertaining distraction.

Also - it sounds weird to say it, but yes I'm a human and every word on this blog was written by me! LLMs are remarkable, but they have no place on a personal blog.
